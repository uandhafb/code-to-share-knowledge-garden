## How it works

Angle brackets hold a list of options, and each cycle plays the next one. Here, the second step is `sd`, then `cp`, then `rim`, then `sd` again.

## Example

```strudel
s("bd <sd cp rim>")
```

## Try this

Two steps taking turns:

```strudel
s("<bd jazz> <sd cp>")
```
