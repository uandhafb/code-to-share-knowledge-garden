Every pattern starts with sounds. Paste an example into the [Strudel REPL](https://strudel.cc/), or use its **Open in Strudel** link, then press **Ctrl + Enter** to play or update it and **Ctrl + .** to stop. On a Mac, both use **Control**, not Command.

Open the **sounds** tab in the REPL to see and hear every sound.
