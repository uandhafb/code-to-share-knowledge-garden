A **scale** picks a few notes out of all the possible ones. Using only those notes makes a melody sound like it lives in one musical world. Some notes feel stable, and others feel like they want to move: tension and release. Major often sounds brighter and minor darker (a common feeling, not a rule).

Playing together? Agree on a tonic and a scale first, and everything will fit.
