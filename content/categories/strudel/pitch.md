`note("...")` plays pitches. You can write them as numbers or as letters, and the mini-notation symbols work with notes too.

One octave of the piano:

```text
letter:  c   c#  d   d#  e   f   f#  g   g#  a   a#  b   c
         .   db  .   eb  .   .   gb  .   ab  .   bb  .   .
number:  60  61  62  63  64  65  66  67  68  69  70  71  72
key:     W   B   W   B   W   W   B   W   B   W   B   W   W
```

W is a white key and B is a black key.
