## This garden's background

A soft gradient made with Hydra: very slow, very wide stripes, turned on their side and softened. Change a number and press **Run** (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter):

```hydra
osc(4.5, 0.004, 2.1)
  .rotate(-1.57)
  .saturate(0.6)
  .brightness(0.3)
  .out()
```

If the motion bothers you, use **Pause visuals**. **Hide text** lets you watch the visuals on their own.
